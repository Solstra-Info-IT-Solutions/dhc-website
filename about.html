Who we are

Dream House Cinemas is a Kerala based film production and equipment rental company dedicated to turning ideas into powerful visual stories. With a passion for storytelling and a commitment to excellence, we create content that connects, inspires and leaves a lasting impact.

From concept development to final delivery, we handle every aspect of production with creativity, precision and professionalism. Our state-of-the-art equipment and skilled team ensure world-class quality for films, commercials, documentaries, corporate videos, and digital content.

We believe in building strong relationships with our clients by understanding their vision and delivering beyond expectations. Whether it’s a small shoot or a large-scale production, we bring the same level of dedication and quality to every project.

At Dream House Cinemas, your story is our passion.
